## Direction contract

THESIS: One dark stage, one faceted obsidian form that follows the reader through every section, and type large enough to be the scenery. It refuses the card-grid developer portfolio (avatar, skill bars, project cards).

OWN-WORLD: #0a0a0a ground, #f5f5f0 type, #c6ff3d used only as a signal (cursor dot, active timeline node, progress hairline, primary CTA hover, "available" dot). Clash Display set huge and tight; Satoshi body. Media has hard 0-radius edges; small controls are pills; inputs are bottom rules. Canvas grain at 6% opacity.

STORY: Who he is (name, role), what he believes about engineering (About scrub), what he works with (Skills index), where he has been (timeline), moments (Gallery), proof (Projects with repos), then one email address set as large as a headline.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name in two lines at ~13vw anchored bottom-left; role line and 18-word subtext under it; primary "View Selected Work" pill plus contact text link; WebGL form on the right half reacting to the pointer; bottom row: location, availability dot, scroll cue.

FORM: Brief-pinned direction (no concept-seed roll; owner pinned mood, palette, fonts, stack). Code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
