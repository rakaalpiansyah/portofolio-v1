## Product Principles

1. Show the shipped thing (repo, store listing, live docs) before describing it.
2. Every number on the page comes from the owner's own data.
3. Recruiters must reach contact details within one scroll gesture from any section (navbar).
4. Motion serves reading order; reduced-motion users get the full content without it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, full keyboard operation, visible focus, `prefers-reduced-motion` honored.
